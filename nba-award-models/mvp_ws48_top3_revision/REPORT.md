# MVP top-three eligibility: WS/48 strictly above 0.200

## Rules

Players must rank in the top 25 in points per game among players with at least 20 appearances, including ties at 25th. To occupy positions 1, 2 or 3, they must additionally have raw WS/48 strictly greater than 0.200. A value of exactly 0.200 does not qualify; missing WS/48 also does not qualify.

Select the three highest-scoring qualifying players for positions 1-3. Then rank every remaining top-25-PPG candidate from fourth onward by the existing score. The scoring coefficients, season-specific fits, 85% availability penalty and score contributions are unchanged. Constraints may place a lower-scoring qualified player above a higher-scoring player who cannot enter the top three. If fewer than three players qualify, the unfillable top-three positions stay vacant. All 11 evaluated seasons have at least three qualifiers.

## Shares and validation

model_share is softmax over all top-25-PPG players who satisfy WS/48 >0.200, because only they can rank first. Other players receive zero first-place share. These are uncalibrated model shares. previous_score_share retains the previous softmax values across all top-25 PPG candidates. Eligibility filtering changes denominators mechanically; it does not improve underlying score calibration.

Every underlying seasonal score was fitted using earlier seasons only. The hard ranking rules were designed after inspecting historical results, so the comparisons remain retrospective. No retraining or coefficient selection occurred in this step.

Run `python3 ../rank_mvp_ws48_top3.py`. Checks cover the strict 0.200 boundary, retained candidate count, unchanged score contributions, top-25 PPG qualification, all 33 top-three WS/48 qualifications, complete season results and model-share normalization. Full rankings and raw WS/48 are in all_eligible_rankings.csv. The PDF remains on its earlier revision.
